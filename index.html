01 / Research profile

Mathematical structure in learning systems.

A research path from applied machine learning toward questions about how models represent relations, geometry, and uncertainty in scientific data.

Research statement

My previous work has centered on structured data: graph-based relational models for object-centric processes, geometric deep learning for 3D point clouds, and representation learning for gravitational-lensing images. These projects have made me interested in the mathematical structure behind useful representations.

At AIMS, I am developing the mathematical and scientific foundations to investigate that structure more carefully. I am especially interested in geometric and topological learning, probabilistic methods, and their applications to scientific problems. These are directions I am building toward, alongside experience I have already gained in geometric and graph-based learning.

3D geometric learning · Graduation research

AI-automated dental crown generation

Developed a 3D pipeline for tooth segmentation and crown generation, exploring FoldingNet, DGCNN, point-cloud transformers, and contrastive learning. The work focused on preserving geometric structure and maintaining upper and lower dental arches in a canonical space.

Explore the geometric learning work View repository

Scientific imaging · DeepLense GSoC 2025

Representation learning for gravitational lenses

Pretrained a masked autoencoder on strong-lensing images, then explored downstream classification and super-resolution. The project combined scientific image data with self-supervised representation learning and quantitative image-quality evaluation.

The detailed project report records approximately 97% classification accuracy and validation super-resolution results of 29.62 dB PSNR and 0.919 SSIM.

Explore the methods and results View repository

3D reconstruction · 3DIVE

Reconstructing real environments

Built a reconstruction pipeline combining COLMAP camera pose estimation, Gaussian Splatting, and SuGaR to create interactive 3D scenes from real-estate video.
