Research focus

Geometric deep learning

Learning directly from 3D structure — point clouds, meshes, surfaces — rather than flattening it away. Applied to segmenting and generating dental anatomy with FoldingNet, DGCNN and Point Cloud Transformers, and to gravitational-lens classification with masked autoencoders.

Graph & process-centric learning

Modelling how entities and events relate as graphs rather than tables. Built graph-based relational networks for anomaly detection in object-centric business processes, and reinforcement-learning methods for streaming process discovery under concept drift.

Applied mathematics for structural problems

Increasingly drawn to the mathematics underneath the models — topology, geometry, inference — as tools for reasoning about complex structures at scale, from molecules to planetary systems.
